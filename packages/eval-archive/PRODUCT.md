# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Evaluation engineers and agent researchers who are running a benchmark and need to understand its health while it is still in progress. They move from the bench overview to a task, then to an individual trail and the agent conversation that produced it.

## Product Purpose

The eval workspace turns benchmark events and metric streams into an inspectable visual surface. Success means a researcher can spot a regression, identify the task and trail responsible, and inspect the agent's reasoning without losing the surrounding statistical context.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, exacting, quietly technical. The visual reference is the Zed website's precise desktop geometry and calm work surface: confident, crisp, and made for sustained attention rather than spectacle.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboard layouts built from identical shadow-heavy cards, gradient/glow decoration, oversized marketing copy, playful AI-chat tropes, and dense control chrome that competes with the charts.

## Design Principles

- Charts are the primary visual language; supporting copy and controls stay quiet.
- Preserve the evidence trail from bench aggregate to task, trail, and agent conversation.
- Use a disciplined geometry and material vocabulary so the interface feels like one instrument.
- Keep evaluation state, time, and selection visible enough for reproducible investigation.
- Reduce cognitive noise around active analysis without hiding useful context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the interface. Do not encode metric meaning by color alone; pair chart colors with labels, patterns, or direct annotations. All navigation, tabs, task selection, and trail selection must be keyboard reachable with visible focus states. Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain usable layouts on narrow screens.
